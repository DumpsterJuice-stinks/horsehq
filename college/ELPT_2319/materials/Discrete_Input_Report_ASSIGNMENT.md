## The instructor's EXAMPLE row (do not copy — it's the model)

**Ultrasonic switch** — link: alllaboutcircuits.com/industry-articles/understanding-the-basics-of-ultrasonic-proximity-sensors/

- **Definition given:** "An ultrasonic switch is a device that senses objects by
  first directing ultrasound in a certain direction, then it detects the the
  ultrasound that returns to the switch when an object interrupts the pathway."
- **Principle of operation:** a diagram showing ultrasonic transmitter/receiver,
  measuring distance, actual distance, and obstacle.
- **Industrial application given:** hazardous materials stored in tanks — instead
  of a float switch that would get contaminated, use ultrasonic because "only
  sound touches the process"; mount vertically to sense rising level, calibrated
  to switch at a certain height; a technician replacing it has little danger of
  touching the dangerous material.
- **Example for purchase:** FLOWLINE – EchoSwitch® LU74-78 Ultrasonic Level
  Switch & Controller; LU74-5004, 5.5 m (18′) range, 95–250 VAC, 2″ NPT,
  **$1,100.00** — with a product photo.

Note the application cell is where the instructor writes the most: it explains
