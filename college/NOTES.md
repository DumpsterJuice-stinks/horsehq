- **Instructor:** David Loveless — dloveless@lee.edu — no office, no office phone

- **Official communication:** Blackboard announcements and Blackboard messages.

- **Blackboard course:** elearn.lee.edu/ultra/courses/_37784_1
- **Header on printout:** "ELPTB 2319 2371" (section shows as H03A on the title)

**Description:** Fundamental concepts of programmable logic controllers,
principles of operation, and numbering systems as applied to electrical controls.

**Learning outcomes:**
1. Identify and describe digital logic circuits and explain numbering systems;
   explain the operation of PLCs; convert ladder diagrams into programs;
   incorporate timers and counters using PLCs.
2. Execute and evaluate programs.

**Textbook:** *Programmable Logic Controllers*, Frank Petruzella, McGraw Hill,
6th ed. — ISBN 9781264163342 — **required, ETEXTBOOK**

**Late work policy:** **No penalty for late work.**

## Shared Lee College Policies (both courses)

- **AI policy — identical in both syllabi: "AI Encouraged with Attribution,
  Verification and Reflection."** Using AI to find information, brainstorm, and
  test arguments is *encouraged and expected*. Copying/paraphrasing from an AI
  assistant is permitted as long as copied material is quoted or in a different
  colored font **with a citation**, and paraphrased material is cited. Citations
  must include verification — identify the facts/sources/quotes and confirm they
  were independently verified as correct/real. Principles: AI should help you
  think, not think for you; you are responsible for accuracy; don't enter info
  you wouldn't post on social media; **you must disclose generative AI use**.
- **myBooks:** non-dual-credit students auto-enrolled; $33.00 per semester credit
  hour; opt out via myLC Campus. Contact myBooks@lee.edu.
- **Hybrid format:** half the coursework independently on Blackboard, half in
  face-to-face meetings; labs are done in the face-to-face sessions.
- **Attendance:** notify instructor by phone or email when unable to attend.
  Religious holy day absences need a week's prior notice.
- **Incomplete ("I"):** requires ≥75% of coursework completed plus a contract
  with the instructor; remaining work due within one semester or it becomes an F.
- **Withdrawal:** student's responsibility; Six Drop Rule may apply; resignation
  = W4 in all courses.
