# TheHorseHQ — Facebook Group Post Prompt

A reusable prompt for generating short, punchy posts for equestrian Facebook groups.
Fill the bracketed slots, paste into any capable LLM, pick your favourite variant.

---

## The prompt

```
You are writing a Facebook post for an equestrian Facebook group.

THE PRODUCT
TheHorseHQ — [one line: what it is]
What it actually does for a horse person: [2–3 concrete benefits, plain language]
Price / free tier: [e.g. free to browse, £X/mo to list]
Link: [URL]

THE GROUP
Name: [group name]
Who's in it: [e.g. UK amateur eventers, 20k members, mostly women 25–55]
Group vibe: [e.g. chatty and casual / strictly buy-sell / heavily moderated]
Self-promo rules: [allowed / Fridays only / must be a member first / banned]

WRITE THE POST
Constraints:
- 60–90 words. Mobile screens cut off at ~3 lines — earn the "See more" click.
- Open with a hook that names a real, specific frustration this audience has.
  No "Attention horse owners!", no "Are you tired of...", no rhetorical throat-clearing.
- Sound like a person in the group, not a brand. Contractions, short sentences,
  one idea per line. Write at a 7th-grade reading level.
- Say what it does before you say what it's called.
- Exactly one call to action. One link, on its own line at the end.
- No emoji walls (max 1–2, or none). No hashtag stacks. No "DM me!!".
- Never claim results, prices, or features that aren't in THE PRODUCT above.
- If the group bans self-promo, write it as a genuinely useful comment/value post
  that mentions the product once, in passing, and flag that it needs mod approval.

Give me 3 variants with different angles:
1. Problem-first — lead with the pain, land on the fix.
2. Story//first-person — "I built this because..." or "I kept seeing people ask..."
3. Straight and useful — no hook games, just the clearest possible statement of value.

For each: label the angle, then the post text only. No commentary.
Then add one line: the single riskiest word or claim in each, and why.
```

---

## Filling it in — what actually matters

| Slot | Get this wrong and… |
|---|---|
| **Concrete benefits** | Vague input gives vague output. "Manage your horse business" → useless. "See every vet, farrier and feed cost per horse in one place" → usable. |
| **Group vibe** | A post that works in a chatty local group reads as spam in a strict sales group. |
| **Self-promo rules** | This is the one that gets you banned. Check pinned posts before you write, not after. |

## Worked example

> **Assumption:** TheHorseHQ is an app for tracking horse care and costs — vet, farrier,
> feed, competition entries — in one place, replacing the notes-app-and-shoebox method.
> Swap this for the real product brief before using any of the copy below.

**1. Problem-first**

> Farrier's due and you can't remember if it's been five weeks or eight. The vet asks
> when she was last wormed and you're scrolling back through a year of texts.
>
> That's the whole reason we built TheHorseHQ. Every date, every cost, every horse —
> one place, on your phone at the yard.
>
> Free to try, no card needed.
>
> thehorsehq.com

**2. Story / first-person**

> I kept a shoebox of farrier receipts for four years. When I finally added it up I'd
> spent more on shoes than on the horse.
>
> So I built the thing I wanted: log a cost in ten seconds, see what each horse actually
> costs you a month. It's called TheHorseHQ.
>
> Happy to answer anything in the comments.
>
> thehorsehq.com

**3. Straight and useful**

> TheHorseHQ is an app for tracking horse care — vet visits, farrier, worming, feed,
> entries — per horse, with the costs attached.
>
> Reminders before things are due. Export the lot if you need it for insurance or a sale.
>
> Free tier, works offline at the yard.
>
> thehorsehq.com

**Riskiest bits:** "no card needed" and "works offline" are checkable claims — only keep
them if they're true. "Free to try" is safer than "Free" if the trial has a limit.

## Before you post

- **Read the pinned rules.** Most horse groups ban promo outright or fence it to a
  weekly thread. Posting into a ban gets the link domain blocked, not just the post.
- **Message the mods first** for the groups you care about. A yes from a mod is worth
  more than a clever hook.
- **Don't paste the same text into both groups.** Facebook demotes duplicate content
  across groups, and members overlap more than you'd think.
- **Comment for the first hour.** Group reach dies fast without replies.
