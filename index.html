Frequently Asked

How do I pay?

You'll be taken straight to a secure Stripe checkout in a new tab right after you submit — no waiting on an email.

Once payment is confirmed (usually within minutes during business hours), your listing goes live automatically.

Can I edit my listing?

Yes. Email changes to edit@thehorsehq.com with your listing ID in the subject line.

No refunds once payment is processed and listing is live. If there's an error on our end, we'll fix it immediately.
