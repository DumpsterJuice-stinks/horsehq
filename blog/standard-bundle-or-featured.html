Standard, Bundle, or Featured: Which Listing Option Is Right for You?

The Horse HQ Team · August 2026

Every seller's situation is different, and the right listing setup depends more on what you're actually selling than on which option sounds like the best deal. Here's how to think through it.

Standard Listing — One Horse, One Sale

If you're selling a single horse, Standard is the default for a reason: $50 gets you a full 30-day listing with photo support, category placement, and your contact info in front of buyers actively browsing that category. There's no reason to overcomplicate a single sale — list it, let it run, and boost it later if it needs more visibility than it's getting organically.

Bundle — When You're Moving Several at Once

Ranches, trainers, and breeders clearing out multiple horses in the same season are the clear use case here. Bundle gets you up to 4 listings for $160 — a real savings over buying them individually — and you can mix categories freely, so a yearling, a finished gelding, and a load of hay can all go under one purchase. The math only works in your favor once you're listing 4 at a time; for 1-2 horses, Standard is still cheaper per listing.

Boost — A Fast Nudge, Not a Long-Term Strategy

Boost moves an existing listing to the top of its category page for $30. It's the right call when a listing has been sitting for a while and you think it's a visibility problem, not a pricing or presentation problem. It's not a substitute for a listing with weak photos or a thin description — boosting a listing that isn't converting just gets more people to see the same problem faster.

Featured — Homepage Placement, For When It Matters Most

Featured puts your listing in front of every visitor who lands on the homepage, for 7 days, for $200. It makes the most sense for a genuinely standout horse — one with a real story, strong pedigree, or proven performance record — where the extra exposure is likely to reach buyers who wouldn't have found it by browsing categories alone. For a mid-range horse in a niche category, the category page traffic is probably already your best-fit audience, and Featured may not pay for itself.

A Simple Way to Decide

  • Selling one horse → Standard
  • Selling four or more at once → Bundle
  • A good listing that isn't getting seen → Boost
  • A standout horse worth the extra reach → Featured

None of these are exclusive — a common pattern is Standard or Bundle to get listed, then Boost or Featured later if it needs a push. You don't have to decide everything up front.
